The Compact
We built this tool on the same principles that governed the fairest ships on the sea — transparency in every process, no central authority, and every hand sovereign over their own treasure.
I. Your data never leaves your device. No server, no cloud, no third-party databases.
II. No accounts, no tracking. We don't know who you are and we don't want to.
III. Open, portable formats. Export anytime. Your collection is yours to take wherever you sail.
IV. Transparent code. Every plank of this ship is visible. No hidden compartments.
